How it works:

Loads your image via GDI+ (PNG, BMP, JPG, whatever — same trick as before, no external libraries).
If it isn't already exactly 32×32, resizes it (bicubic). For crisp results with something like your star, save it at exactly 32×32 in Photoshop so no resampling happens at all.
Thresholds every pixel to black/white by luminance (default cutoff 128 — dark pixels become "on"/lit). Transparent pixels always count as background.
Packs it into the identical uint32_t[32] row format your font array uses, so it's a drop-in graphics32x32[][32] PROGMEM array.

cl /EHsc /std:c++17 image_to_arduino.cpp

image_to_arduino.exe icons.h star.png
image_to_arduino.exe icons.h star.png heart.png arrow.png    (multiple entries in one array)
image_to_arduino.exe icons.h star.png 100                    (custom threshold)
image_to_arduino.exe icons.h star.png invert                 (light shape on dark background)
